Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025

1. What this app is

Amethyst is a personal, single-user Android application built by an individual student developer for their own use. It is not distributed publicly, is not available on the Play Store, and has no users other than its developer.

It has exactly one function: automatically uploading one pre-made vertical video per day to the developer's own YouTube channel at a scheduled time. All videos are pre-recorded and owned by the developer.

2. What data it accesses

Amethyst accesses the YouTube Data API v3 using a single OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload

This scope is granted by the developer's own Google account and is used solely to upload videos to that same account's channel. The app does not read, analyze, or store any YouTube data beyond the video ID returned after a successful upload.

No data is collected from any user other than the developer, because there are no other users. The app does not use cookies, analytics, trackers, or advertising.

3. What data it stores

The only data Amethyst stores is a local checklist kept on the developer's own device, containing:

  • the filename of each video that has already been uploaded;
  • the video ID returned by YouTube after each successful upload.

This checklist exists only to avoid uploading the same video twice. It is stored locally on the device. Nothing is stored on a server, in the cloud, or anywhere outside the device.

4. What data it shares

Nothing. No data is shared, sold, rented, published, or transferred to anyone.

5. Third parties

The only third party involved is Google / YouTube, through the YouTube Data API v3, which is used to perform the upload. No other third-party services, SDKs, servers, or advertising networks are used.

Google's handling of data is described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. YouTube API Services

Amethyst uses YouTube API Services and complies with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. Use of YouTube through this app is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and to Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Data retention and deletion

The only stored data is the local checklist described above, which lives on the developer's device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no server-side copy and no account to delete.

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
