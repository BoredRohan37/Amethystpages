Amethyst

A personal, single-user Android application.

Amethyst is a personal, single-user Android application built by an individual student developer for their own use. It has exactly one function: automatically uploading one pre-made vertical video per day to the developer's own YouTube channel at a scheduled time. All videos are pre-recorded and owned by the developer.

The app is not distributed publicly, is not available on the Play Store, and has no users other than its developer. Because there are no third parties, it does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data belonging to anyone else. It does not use cookies, analytics, trackers, or advertising.

Amethyst is a personal project, not a commercial product.
